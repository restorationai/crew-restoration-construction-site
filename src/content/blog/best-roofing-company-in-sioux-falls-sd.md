## What Should You Look for in a Sioux Falls Roofing Company?

The right roofing company for a South Dakota roof carries a current state contractor license, documents the scope of work before starting, and can explain how hail and wind damage gets handled with your insurer.

Sioux Falls sits in a part of the state that sees regular hail and severe wind events, so [roof resilience to impact and wind uplift](https://ibhs.org/) is worth asking about directly, not assuming. Ask any contractor what shingle class they're installing, how they're flashing valleys and penetrations, and whether they'll provide a written estimate broken out by material and labor before you sign anything.

Material choice matters too. Standard three-tab and architectural asphalt shingles are the default across most Sioux Falls neighborhoods, but some homeowners weigh a [metal roof](/blog/pros-and-cons-of-a-metal-roof/) for its longer service life against the higher upfront cost. If your roof was hit by hail, read up on [who to call first for hail damage](/blog/who-to-call-for-hail-damage-in-sioux-falls-sd/) before you let anyone climb a ladder, since the order of calls can affect your claim.

## Will Homeowners Insurance Cover My Roof Replacement?

Most South Dakota homeowners policies cover roof damage from a sudden, covered peril like hail or wind, but they typically exclude damage caused by age, wear, or deferred maintenance.

An adjuster will usually want dated photos of the damage, a written repair estimate, and documentation that you mitigated further loss (tarping an active leak, for example) before approving a claim. [Coverage specifics vary by policy and insurer](https://www.iii.org/article/which-disasters-are-covered-by-homeowners-insurance), so check your declarations page for wind/hail deductibles, which are often a separate percentage-based deductible rather than your standard flat amount.
