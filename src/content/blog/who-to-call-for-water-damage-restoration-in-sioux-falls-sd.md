Call a water damage restoration company first, not your insurance company. Restoration crews stop the damage from spreading. Insurance adjusters document what already happened. Every hour of delay lets water migrate further into walls, subfloor, and framing, and mold can begin colonizing wet materials within 24 to 48 hours according to the [EPA's guidance on mold and moisture](https://www.epa.gov/mold).

## When to Call Someone Else Before the Restoration Company

Three situations require a different first call.

**The water source is still running and you cannot shut it off.** If a pipe has burst and you cannot reach the main shutoff, call a licensed plumber first. The restoration crew cannot safely extract water that is still actively flowing from a pressurized line. Once the source is controlled, call Crew at (605) 965-2727 to begin extraction and drying.

**There is an electrical hazard.** Water near your breaker panel, submerged outlets, or sparking wiring is a life-safety issue. Call 911 or your utility provider (Northwestern Energy in most of Sioux Falls) before anyone enters the affected area. Once the power is confirmed safe, the restoration crew takes over.

**You smell gas.** Leave the building immediately, do not flip any switches, and call 911 and your gas utility from outside. Water damage sometimes shifts gas lines in older homes. This is not a restoration call until the scene is safe.

For every other water damage scenario, including appliance leaks, toilet overflows, storm intrusion, sewage backup, and frozen pipe thaws, the restoration company is your first call.

## What Happens When You Call Crew Restoration & Construction

Knowing what to expect on that first call reduces the stress of an already stressful situation.

When you reach Crew at **(605) 965-2727**, a team member will ask the intake questions above and schedule a crew dispatch. On arrival, the technicians perform a moisture assessment using calibrated meters and thermal imaging to map exactly where water has traveled, including inside wall cavities and under flooring that looks dry on the surface.

From there, the process follows the [IICRC S500 Standard for Professional Water Damage Restoration](https://www.iicrc.org), the industry's governing document for drying protocols:

1. **Water extraction** using truck-mounted or portable extraction units to remove standing water
2. **Structural drying** with commercial-grade LGR dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers positioned to dry materials from the inside out
3. **Moisture monitoring** with daily readings logged to document drying progress for your insurance file
4. **Antimicrobial treatment** applied to affected surfaces to inhibit mold growth during the drying window
5. **Scope documentation** with photos, moisture logs, and a written scope of work before any reconstruction begins

The drying phase typically runs three to five days for a standard residential loss, though Category 3 water (sewage or floodwater) and larger affected areas take longer. ## When Do I Call My Insurance Company?

Call your insurance company after the restoration crew has assessed the damage and started mitigation, not before. Here is why: your policy requires you to mitigate promptly. Waiting for an adjuster to arrive before starting drying can void coverage for secondary damage (mold, structural rot) that develops during the delay.

Crew Restoration & Construction handles the documentation that protects your claim: moisture readings, photo logs, a written scope, and direct communication with your adjuster. If you want a plain-English breakdown of what South Dakota homeowners policies typically cover for water damage, the post [Does Homeowners Insurance Cover Water Damage?](/blog/does-homeowners-insurance-cover-water-damage/) walks through the most common scenarios.

One practical note: sudden, accidental losses (burst pipe, appliance failure, storm intrusion through a damaged roof) are almost always covered. Slow leaks and gradual seepage are almost always excluded. Document everything before cleanup begins.

## What If There Is Mold After the Water Damage?

Mold can begin growing within 24 to 48 hours on wet organic materials like drywall, wood framing, and carpet padding. If the water damage was not caught quickly, or if a slow leak went undetected for weeks, mold remediation may be needed alongside or after the drying work.
