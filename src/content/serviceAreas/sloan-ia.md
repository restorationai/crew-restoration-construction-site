## Restoration emergencies common in Sloan

Sloan sits in Woodbury County along the Missouri River floodplain, and that low-lying position shapes the kind of calls property owners here tend to make. Heavy spring rain and snowmelt can push groundwater up against foundations faster than older drain tile can handle, and basements with limited sump capacity are often the first place water shows up. Iowa winters bring freeze-thaw cycles that stress aging pipe joints and roof flashing, which is why burst pipes and ice-related roof leaks are common in the colder months. Summer storms moving through the river valley also bring straight-line wind and hail that can strip shingles or drive water behind siding, setting up conditions for mold if it isn't dried out quickly.
