## Restoration emergencies common in Ponca

Ponca sits close to the Missouri River, and spring snowmelt combined with heavy rain events can push groundwater and surface water toward low-lying lots faster than older drainage systems were built to handle. Basements and crawlspaces take on water during these periods, and once moisture is trapped under flooring or behind drywall, mold can begin colonizing within 24 to 48 hours. Nebraska winters bring hard freezes that crack older galvanized or copper supply lines, and a pipe that fails overnight in an unoccupied outbuilding or seasonal cabin can run for hours before anyone notices. Severe thunderstorms and the occasional tornado track through this part of northeast Nebraska in late spring and summer, which is when we see the most calls for roof damage, wind-driven water intrusion, and board-up needs after a window or siding failure.
