## Restoration emergencies common in Meadow View Addition

Southeastern South Dakota runs through a wide temperature swing every year, and that swing is what drives most of the restoration calls in this part of the state. Winter brings hard freezes that split supply lines and crack fittings in homes with marginal insulation, and the resulting leaks often go unnoticed until a ceiling stain or a soft spot in the flooring shows up. Spring thaw and heavy rain events push groundwater against foundations and into crawlspaces and basements, especially in older homes where exterior drainage was never upgraded. Summer storms bring wind and hail that damage roofing and siding, opening the building envelope to water intrusion long after the storm itself has passed. Each of these patterns behaves differently depending on how a home was built and where the water actually travels once it gets inside.
