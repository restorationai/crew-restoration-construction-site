## Restoration emergencies common in Lawton

Lawton sits in the Missouri and Big Sioux River drainage basin, where heavy spring rain and snowmelt push groundwater levels up and strain older storm sewers and farm drainage tile that was never sized for today's rainfall totals. Basements and crawlspaces in town take on water through foundation cracks and sump failures during these wet stretches, and summer thunderstorms bring wind and hail that damage roofing and siding on short notice. Winter adds its own risk: hard freezes crack exposed or poorly insulated supply lines, and a burst pipe in an unoccupied outbuilding or farmhouse can run for days before anyone notices the damage.

## Services we provide in Lawton

Water damage restoration and emergency water removal are our most common calls here, usually tied to basement flooding or a failed sump pump during a heavy rain event.
