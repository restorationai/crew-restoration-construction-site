## Restoration emergencies common in Sergeant Bluff

Sergeant Bluff sits in the Missouri River floodplain in Woodbury County, and that geography shapes the damage we see. Heavy spring rain and snowmelt push the water table up fast, which means sump pumps run harder and basements take on water even when the storm itself has passed. Homes built on slab or shallow crawlspace foundations near the lower-lying areas of town are more exposed to seepage after a hard rain than those on higher ground. Winters bring deep freeze cycles that stress older galvanized and copper supply lines, and a burst pipe in an unheated garage or crawlspace is one of the more common calls this region generates once temperatures swing back above freezing. Grain and ag-industry traffic through the area also means commercial and light-industrial buildings along the Highway 75 corridor carry their own fire and equipment-failure risk.
