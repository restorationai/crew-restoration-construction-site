## Restoration emergencies common in Walthill

Walthill sits in Thurston County along the Missouri River valley, and that geography shapes the kinds of calls property owners here deal with. Heavy spring rain and snowmelt push water into basements and crawlspaces faster than older drainage systems were designed to handle, and a lot of the housing stock predates modern sump and grading standards. Winters bring hard freezes that split supply lines inside unheated crawlspaces, and a burst pipe discovered after a cold snap can mean standing water has been sitting under flooring for days before anyone notices the smell. Grass and brush fires in the surrounding farmland also bring smoke damage calls that have nothing to do with a structure fire on the property itself.
