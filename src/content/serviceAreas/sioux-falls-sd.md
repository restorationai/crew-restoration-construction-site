## Restoration emergencies common in Sioux Falls

Much of Sioux Falls was built out in waves from the postwar period through the 1970s and 80s, with older pockets near Falls Park, McKennan Park, and the Cathedral Historic District dating to the early 1900s and carrying original brick, plaster walls, and in some cases galvanized or cast iron plumbing that's now well past its service life.
