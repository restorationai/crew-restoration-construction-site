## Restoration emergencies common in Lyons

Lyons sits in McCook County farm country, where winters bring deep frost and spring brings fast snowmelt and heavy rain across flat, slow-draining ground. That combination is hard on older plumbing and foundations: frozen supply lines crack during cold snaps, sump pumps get overwhelmed when thaw and rainfall hit together, and basements built decades ago take on water through foundation cracks that only show themselves under pressure. Grain storage, outbuildings, and older farmhouses also mean fire risk from electrical systems and heating equipment that weren't built for today's load. Because many properties here are spread out on acreage rather than clustered in a subdivision, a small leak or chimney fire can go unnoticed longer before anyone's home to catch it.

## Services we provide in Lyons

Water damage in Lyons often starts below grade, in a basement or crawlspace that's been fed by snowmelt or a cracked line, which means we're pulling flooring and checking framing moisture, not just running fans over a wet carpet. Fire and smoke damage calls typically involve older wiring, wood stoves, or grain-drying and farm equipment near structures, so cleanup includes soot and odor work on both the home and attached outbuildings. Biohazard and trauma cleanup, and storm or wind damage from the open plains weather this part of the state sees, round out the emergency side, while our reconstruction crews handle the drywall, flooring, and framing repair once the structure is dry and the cause is fixed.
