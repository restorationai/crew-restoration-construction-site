## Restoration emergencies common in Wakefield

Wakefield sits in Nebraska's northeast corner, where spring snowmelt and heavy thunderstorm rainfall push area creeks and low-lying fields toward saturation faster than many homeowners expect. Older basements and crawlspaces in town take on groundwater during wet stretches, and sump pumps that haven't been serviced in a while tend to fail at the worst possible moment. Winter brings its own version of the same problem: a hard freeze followed by a quick thaw is a classic setup for a burst supply line in an uninsulated crawlspace or an exterior wall that was never built for today's cold snaps. Add in the occasional severe thunderstorm or hail event that rolls through the region, and roof damage, wind-driven water intrusion, and the mold growth that follows an undetected leak round out the calls we expect to see in this part of Nebraska.
