## Our Flood Damage Restoration Process in Hawarden

The first visit is about stopping the damage from spreading, not just pumping out what's visible. We extract standing water, then use moisture meters to map how far it has traveled into drywall, subfloor, and framing, because floodwater in a Hawarden basement rarely stays where you can see it. Drying follows the IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration: air movers and dehumidifiers are placed based on the moisture readings, not guesswork, and we recheck daily until materials read dry rather than just dry to the touch. If the flooding involved river water or backed-up ground water, we treat it as contaminated and handle demolition of affected drywall, insulation, and flooring accordingly, since porous materials exposed to floodwater generally can't be fully sanitized and dried in place. Throughout the job we document everything with photos and moisture logs, which matters when a claim moves through an insurance adjuster.
