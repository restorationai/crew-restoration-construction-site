## Our Basement Flooding Cleanup Process in Brookings

The first visit is about stopping the loss and figuring out what's salvageable. We extract standing water, pull baseboards and any saturated drywall below the waterline, and check what's behind the finished surfaces, since block and poured concrete foundations common in Brookings basements hold moisture differently than framed walls upstairs. Moisture readings on the slab and framing tell us whether we're dealing with a surface issue or water that's already worked into the subfloor above. Drying follows the IICRC S500 structural drying standard: air movers and dehumidifiers sized to the square footage and the amount of water involved, with daily moisture checks until the materials read dry, not just the room feeling dry to the touch.
