## Why Rock Valley Properties See Flood Damage Issues

Rock Valley sits along the Rock River, and the flat farmland terrain surrounding the city means water has nowhere fast to drain once the river crests. Properties near Hospers Park and the lower sections closer to the river channel tend to flood first, often before city streets show standing water. Many homes in this area were built with basements intended for storage or utility space rather than finished living areas, which means ductwork, water heaters, and electrical panels sit directly in the flood path. Older homes around Downtown Rock Valley also tend to have clay or cast iron drain lines that back up under pressure, which compounds river flooding with sewer intrusion.

## Our Flood Damage Restoration Process in Rock Valley

We start with extraction, pulling standing water out with truck-mounted pumps before it has time to wick further up drywall and into wall cavities. On river-adjacent properties, we check for sediment and silt left behind once the water recedes since river flooding carries fine soil that settles into carpet padding and subfloor seams and has to be removed separately from the drying process. Moisture mapping comes next, using meters to find where water traveled under flooring and behind baseboards, because floodwater rarely stays where it's visible. Drying follows the IICRC S500 standard, with air movers and dehumidifiers placed to pull moisture out of framing and subfloor materials, not just surface carpet. For basements that took on river water, we also evaluate whether insulation and lower drywall sections need to come out rather than dried in place, since contaminated floodwater carries bacteria that clean groundwater doesn't.

## Rock Valley Insurance Coordination

Flood damage from river overflow is typically excluded from a standard homeowners policy and instead falls under a separate flood insurance policy, which matters for anyone near the Rock River corridor filing a claim for the first time. We document water lines, affected materials, and moisture readings with photos and written notes that match what most flood insurance adjusters need to process a claim, and we can speak directly with the carrier if coverage questions come up mid-job. Homes in the 51247 ZIP code that have flooded before sometimes already carry flood policies through the National Flood Insurance Program, which changes what's covered compared to a standard homeowners claim, so we ask early which policy applies before starting demolition.

## Local note

Homes along the Rock River area with finished or partially finished basements often have sump pumps installed, but during a significant river rise the water table itself climbs faster than a single pump can keep up with, especially if the power goes out during the same storm that caused the flooding. We check for a battery backup or water-powered backup pump on these systems, because a sump pump that stops working during the flood event is one of the most common reasons a manageable few inches of water turns into saturated subfloor and insulation by the time anyone notices.
