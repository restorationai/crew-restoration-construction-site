## Our Lead Paint Abatement Process in Brookings

We start with testing, not assumptions. A property near McCrory Gardens with layered repaints over the decades can test positive in some rooms and negative in others, so we sample trim, window components, and painted plaster or drywall separately rather than treating the whole house as one surface. Once we know where lead is present, we set up containment around the work area, use wet methods and HEPA-filtered extraction instead of dry sanding or open-flame paint removal, and bag and label debris for disposal according to EPA RRP lead-safe work practice requirements. For occupied rentals, we coordinate the schedule so tenants aren't displaced longer than necessary, and we do a visual check plus cleaning verification before we call an area clear.

## Local note

Brookings winters are hard on old exterior paint. Repeated freeze-thaw cycles crack and lift painted wood trim faster than in milder climates, and that cracking releases lead dust into the soil right at the foundation line, not just inside the house. On older properties near Sexauer Park or anywhere with original wood siding, we usually recommend testing the soil perimeter along with interior surfaces, especially if there's a vegetable garden or a play area close to the house.
