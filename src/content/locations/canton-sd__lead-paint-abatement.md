Add in Lincoln County's freeze-thaw winters, which crack old caulk and push moisture behind clapboard, and you get the chalking, flaking paint that shows up on pre-1978 homes long before any renovation is planned.

## Our Lead Paint Abatement Process in Canton

We start with testing, either XRF analysis or lab-submitted paint chip samples, to confirm which surfaces are affected before any disturbance begins. Once lead is confirmed, the work area gets sealed with poly sheeting and negative air containment so dust doesn't migrate into living space or outside toward neighboring yards, which matters on the tighter lots common in older Canton blocks. Removal relies on wet methods, chemical stripping, or controlled HEPA-vacuum sanding rather than dry scraping or open-flame heat guns, both of which are banned under RRP because they aerosolize lead dust. Debris is bagged, labeled, and disposed of as regulated waste, and the area is HEPA-vacuumed and wipe-tested before we call the job clear.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Lead Paint

HEPA-filtered vacuums and air scrubbers stay running throughout the job, not just at cleanup, since lead dust settles quickly but resuspends just as fast if someone walks through it. For exterior work on homes near Newton Hills State Park's more wooded, moisture-heavy lots, we lean on wet scraping and encapsulating primers rather than open sanding, which keeps dust contained even with wind coming off the tree line. Interior trim and window components are often removed intact for off-site stripping when the profile is original to the home, which avoids repeated on-site disturbance of the same painted surface.
