## Our Basement Flooding Cleanup Process in Canton

The first step is always getting standing water out, usually with submersible pumps and truck-mounted extraction, before we assess what's underneath. In a lot of Canton's older basements that means pulling back carpet or subfloor to check framing that's a century old in some cases, since wood from that era absorbs water differently than modern engineered lumber and can look dry on the surface while still holding moisture deep in the grain. We set up air movers and dehumidifiers calibrated to the room's actual size and the building's construction rather than a one-size-fits-all approach, and we check moisture content daily with meters so drying decisions are based on readings, not guesswork. Where foundation walls show evidence of chronic seepage rather than a one-time event, we flag that early so you know whether you're dealing with a cleanup or a longer-term drainage conversation.
