## Why Meadow View Addition Properties See Water Damage Issues

Meadow View Addition sits in a part of South Dakota where frost depth and spring thaw do real work on foundations. Clay-heavy soil common across the region swells when saturated and shrinks when it dries, which over years opens hairline cracks in poured foundations and block walls. Those cracks are invisible until a heavy spring rain or fast snowmelt pushes groundwater against the foundation and it finds the path of least resistance, straight into a finished basement. Add in sump pumps that haven't been tested since the last owner installed them, and a single power outage during a storm can turn a two-inch nuisance into a four-inch structural problem by morning. Homes built on slab or shallow crawlspace foundations, typical of newer subdivisions in this part of the state, have their own failure point: supply line and water heater leaks that run under flooring for days before anyone notices the baseboards are soft.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Meadow View Addition
