## Why Tea Properties See Excavation Challenges

Much of Tea sits on heavy clay subsoil that expands when saturated and contracts hard once it dries out. New builds in the subdivisions pushing west and north of Tea Area High School often need deeper footing excavations than contractors expect if they're used to sandier soil elsewhere in the region. Add in a frost line that regularly reaches well below grade through a South Dakota winter, and shallow digs that worked fine in October can heave by March. Older lots nearer Downtown Tea bring a second problem: decades of informal fill and buried debris from early farmstead use, which we account for before equipment ever breaks ground.

## Our Excavation Process in Tea

Every job starts with an 811 utility locate, non-negotiable given how much new gas, water, and fiber line has gone into the ground around Tea in the last several years as the city has expanded its footprint.
